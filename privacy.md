
# Privacy Policy

## DS218 Google Backup

This is a personal application used to manage Google Takeout backups on a private Synology NAS.

- Google Drive access is authorized through Google OAuth.
- Files are downloaded to the owner's private NAS.
- No personal data is sold or shared with third parties.
- OAuth credentials are stored locally and are not published.
- Google Drive files are deleted only after backup verification.

Access can be revoked through Google Account security settings.
